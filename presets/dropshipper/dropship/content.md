# How my ads and posts should feel

The Ad Creator reads this before writing anything. Edit it whenever you learn what works.

## Style

- UGC style: filmed on a phone, natural light, a real person talking to camera or showing the product.
- First 2 seconds: the problem or the result, never a logo or an intro.
- Short sentences. Casual, friendly, a bit playful. No hype words ("insane", "game-changer").
- Show the product working by second 5.
- Sound-off friendly: captions on screen carry the ad.

## Calls to action

- Ads: "Tap shop now" or "Get yours at the link".
- Organic posts: entertain or teach first; mention the product as part of the story, link in bio.

## Never

- Medical or guaranteed-result claims, fake reviews, fake scarcity, fake discounts.
- Other brands' names.
