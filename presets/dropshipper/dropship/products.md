# What makes a product right for me

The numbers (price range, markup, delivery time, supplier rating) live in `config/attentionos.config.ts` under
`modules.dropship.settings`. This file is for everything else the Product Scout and Product Analyst should know.
Edit it in plain words; every hunt and analysis reads it.

## I like

- Impulse buys under $35 that a 10-second video can show working.
- A visible result: before and after, a satisfying motion, a "how did I live without this".
- Products that solve one clear problem for a clear person.
- Beauty and health tools first (skincare tools, hair tools, self-care, posture and sleep), then anything with a clear winner.
- Light, small and hard to break, so shipping is cheap and returns are rare.

## I avoid

- Anything that needs sizing (clothes, shoes, rings).
- Ingestibles, supplements, creams with active ingredients, or anything that makes a medical claim.
- Branded, licensed or look-alike products.
- Products sold everywhere on the big marketplaces for a few dollars.
- Seasonal products unless the season is coming up.

## Notes for the analyst

- Beauty and health: flag anything that touches the skin or body with a regulated or quality risk, and say what to check.
- When two suppliers sell it, prefer the one with faster delivery to the US over the cheaper one.
