# The kind of store brands I like

The Brand Designer reads this before naming a store or designing a brand.

- Clean and modern, mobile first: most buyers come from a TikTok or Instagram ad on their phone.
- Names: short, brandable, easy to spell after hearing it once. No hyphens or numbers.
- Beauty and health stores: soft, calm palettes with one strong accent colour for buttons.
- One-product stores: the product is the hero; a video at the top of the page.
- Niche stores: a name broad enough for the next ten products in the niche.
- Trust first: delivery times, returns and a support email visible on the product page.
