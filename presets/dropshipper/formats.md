# Video formats that sell products

- **Demo**: the product working, start to finish, no talking needed. Best for satisfying, visual products.
- **Problem and solution**: show the annoying problem for 2 seconds, then the fix.
- **Before and after**: split screen or a cut on the same shot. Only real results.
- **Unboxing and first try**: genuine reaction, real sound.
- **Storytime**: "I bought this because…" with the product in hand.
- **Comparison**: the old way vs this, side by side.
- **POV**: "POV: you finally found…", shot from the user's view.
