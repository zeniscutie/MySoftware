# Dropshipper preset

For dropshippers running niche stores and one-product stores. It turns on the dropshipping teams (`dropship` module)
with these starting rules, which you change any time in `config/attentionos.config.ts`:

- Low-ticket products: sell between $10 and $35, at least 4x markup, delivery within 15 days.
- Beauty and health first, plus any niche with a clear winner.
- Research on TikTok and Meta ads, supplier sites and trends.
- Store model: both niche stores and one-product stores; the Brand Designer recommends one per product.
- Shopify and Meta Ads for store numbers (add the keys to `.env`).

It also copies three playbooks you edit in plain words: `brand/dropship/products.md`, `brand/dropship/content.md`
and `brand/dropship/brand.md`.

Full guide: [docs/dropshipping.md](../../docs/dropshipping.md).
